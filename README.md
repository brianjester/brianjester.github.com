# Brian Jester's Portfolio

Portfolio of projects by Brian Jester in software development, amateur radio, education, investing, and travel. The public homepage is [brianjester.github.io](https://brianjester.github.io/).

## Featured projects

These projects are listed on the portfolio homepage:

- [ValueSlope: Stock Returns & Smoothness](https://brianjester.github.io/value-slope/) - Compare annualized price changes and return smoothness across S&P 500 stocks.
- [Castles of Okinawa: Travel Guide](https://a.co/d/4BS9E7q) - Explore 12 Okinawan castles, with nearby attractions and food recommendations.
- [Fun Filipino Words and Culture: Tagalog-English Children's Book](https://a.co/d/4WbDkpE) - Introduce children to Filipino words and culture with a bilingual Tagalog-English book.
- [IndexFlip: Flashcards & Practice Tests](http://indexflip.com) - Study with flashcards and practice tests for Japanese, amateur radio, driving, music, and real estate.
- [DMR QRZ: Digital Radio Contact Lookup](http://dmrqrz.com) - Find Digital Mobile Radio contacts by radio ID or call sign.
- [Cabrillo Log: Amateur Radio Contest Logger](https://github.com/brianjester/brianjester.github.com/tree/master/cabrillo-log) - Create logs for Amateur Radio contests in Cabrillo format.
- [SOTA Logs: Summits on the Air CSV Logs](https://github.com/brianjester/brianjester.github.com/tree/master/sota-log) - Create and manage CSV logs for Summits on the Air activations.
- [Passive Income: Side-Project Income Chart](https://brianjester.github.io/passive-income/) - View passive income earned from a collection of side projects.
- [Morse Code Key: Browser-Based Morse Tool](https://brianjester.github.io/morse/) - Send Morse code in your browser using your mouse.
- [USGS Earthquake Map: Interactive Map Project](https://github.com/brianjester/myExpressApp) - Explore USGS earthquake data in a map built with Express and Mapbox.
- [Castles of Okinawa: Companion Website](https://brianjester.github.io/okinawa-castles/) - Browse companion information for the travel guide to 12 Okinawan castles.

The homepage also has a placeholder card: **More Projects Coming Soon** - Check back for new software, amateur radio, education, investing, and travel projects.
